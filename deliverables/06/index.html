Portfolio Risk & What-if Analysis

Completed historical and hypothetical shock analysis for a clearly specified duration-only portfolio.

Not client work. Public-source analysis with stated hypothetical inputs where applicable. Not investment advice.

Method

Compute daily bond proxy returns from Treasury yield changes, use an illustrative 20/60/20 split across 5y/10y/20y durations, calculate historical 95% daily VaR/ES and a +100bp parallel duration shock.

Source

https://home.treasury.gov/treasury-daily-interest-rate-xml-feed

Limitations

  • Portfolio and duration assumptions are hypothetical.
  • Historical VaR/ES from duration proxies omit coupons and convexity and do not estimate client portfolio losses.
  • Parallel shock is a first-order sensitivity, not a forecast.
